The studio

Made by hand, one sprite at a time.

Cali Bros is an independent studio building games we'd want to play: readable pixel art, satisfying effects and strategy that rewards a plan.

Every unit, building and boss is designed in-house, and every spell gets its own particle work. We share the process openly, so check back for devlogs and builds.
