Space Fruit

In active development

Somewhere out there grows the fruit of immortality.

Space Fruit is a pixel-art space colony sim. Build your ship room by room, grow strange fruit in its gardens and feed it to a crew with their own skills, moods and needs. Every fruit does something: heal the wounded, sharpen the gunners, keep the engineers awake. Then follow the old star charts, one sector at a time, in search of a tree that never dies.

Primal Man

In active development

Start as a caveman. Build a castle. Rise through the ages.

Primal Man is a top-down multiplayer survival game for phones. Gather wood and stone on a huge island, craft weapons and armor, tame horses, bulls and even elephants, and hire a husky, archer, knight or mage to fight at your side. Your cave grows into a castle with fields around it, and the farther you roam from home, the deadlier the goblins, beasts and bosses get.

RIOT

In active development

Choose your side. Control the street.

RIOT is a crowd-control strategy game you can play from either side of the line. Lead the protesters or command the police through a 50-level campaign across downtown, a college campus, the City Hall plaza, the bridges and the docks. Hundreds of people act on their own, so you don’t give orders, you give suggestions, and not everyone follows. Keep the public on your side, watch the tension, and don’t push the other side past its breaking point.

The studio

Small team. Big sunsets.

Cali Bros is an independent studio building the games we’ve always wanted to play.

We keep the team small and the ideas big. Every project starts with one question: would we stay up all night playing this? If the answer is yes, we build it.
